MediKiosk  🏥

 AI-Powered Patient Case-Taking Software

MediKiosk  is an AI-powered healthcare kiosk for gathering the patient’s medical history before consulting the doctor. It uses **voice interaction, touchscreen input, multilingual support, OCR, and AI** to create structured clinical history for doctors.

📌 Problem

There are many patients that come to the hospital every day, especially OPD departments. There is very little time available for the doctor to gather patient’s history.

Patients always bring the prescribed medications, lab reports, and discharge summaries in paper or picture form. It becomes hard to organize medical information for the doctor.

MediKiosk helps by gathering and organizing patient’s information before consultation.

 💡 Solution

MediKiosk allows patients to:

* Enter their basic details
* Give consent for processing patient’s data
* Answer medical questions by **speaking or touching**
* Choose the language
* Scan or upload previous medical document
* Allow AI to organize their medical information
* Create a structured clinical history for the doctor

🚀 Key Features

🎙️ Voice & Touch Interaction

Allows the patient to answer questions either by speaking or by using the touchscreen.

🌐 Multilingual Capability

Multilingual interaction support in Indian languages for increased accessibility.

🤖 AI-Powered History Taking

AI will pose relevant follow-up questions based on the patient's responses.

📄 Optical Character Recognition of Medical Documents

Prescription, reports, discharge summary can be scanned and converted to digital data.

📋 History Summary

Patient information includes sections like the following:

Chief Complaint
Present Illness History
Medical History
Drug and Allergies History
Family History
Personal History
Systems Review

🚨 Red Flag Detection
The software is capable of detecting any serious symptom and reporting it to hospital personnel.
🩺 Doctor Review
The history of illness that is generated is reviewed by the doctor.
